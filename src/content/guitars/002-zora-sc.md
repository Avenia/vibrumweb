Following the path we started with our first build, this guitar carries the same idea forward: an instrument where every element serves the sound, feel and character of the whole — with a few new twists.

A small jumbo body with a cutaway, spruce over mahogany, and flamed maple running through the neck, fingerboard and pickguard. The same carefully chosen materials, considered details and uncompromising approach to the build, now in a shape that opens up the upper frets.
