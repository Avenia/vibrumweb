Korina is underused in extended-range builds. It's lighter than mahogany, brighter in the upper mids, and it keeps the overall weight of a five-string electric manageable without going to chambering.

The 27-inch scale balances string tension across all five courses. EMG actives keep the noise floor flat and the attack consistent. Luminlay side dots glow in low light — a small practical detail for dark stages.

One of the lighter instruments out of the workshop. Feels different in your hands immediately.
