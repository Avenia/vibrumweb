The Cinderblock 5 is built around a 35-inch scale to keep the B string focused and articulate. The five-piece maple/walnut neck is rigid and resonant, with an asymmetric profile that sits naturally in the hand.

Bartolini MK5 pickups feed a 3-band onboard EQ. The preamp is set conservatively — enough range to shape a tone, not so much that you're fighting it.

This one was commissioned by a session player who needed consistent results across different backlines. It delivered.
