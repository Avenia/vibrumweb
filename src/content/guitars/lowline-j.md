The Lowline J is straightforward on purpose. Alder body, maple neck, maple board — the classic bright-and-punchy combination that defined studio bass for decades.

Lindy Fralin Split Jazz pickups kill the hum without sacrificing the open, glassy character of single-coils. Independent volumes, one tone knob. No preamp, no battery, no switching matrix.

What you dial in is what comes out.
