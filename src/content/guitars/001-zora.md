The first acoustic guitar from the workshop was built around a simple idea: an instrument where every element serves the sound, feel and character of the whole.

Carefully chosen materials, considered details and an uncompromising approach to the build came together in an instrument that found its own voice — setting the foundation for the acoustic guitars to follow.
