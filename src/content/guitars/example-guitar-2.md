The Example Guitar 2 is a working instrument. No binding, no inlays, no surface detail that doesn't earn its place. Ebony fingerboard, satin-finished mahogany body, a neck profile you forget is there after an hour of playing.

The Fishman Fluence Modern pickups cover two distinct voices per position — a wide dynamic range without a battery swap mid-gig. The low B is tight. The high E still sings.

Built for a player who wanted a seven-string that didn't feel like a compromise.
