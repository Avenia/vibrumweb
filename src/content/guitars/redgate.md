The Redgate is currently in the finishing stage. Chambered mahogany body with a spruce top cap — the combination produces a slightly more open, airy response than a solid mahogany slab.

Two Lollar P-90s. The neck position is warm and full without being muddy; the bridge is edgy without tipping into abrasive. Individual volumes allow blend mixing.

Expected completion: mid-2025.
