The Saltmarsh is a commissioned parlour guitar — smaller body, shorter scale, flamed maple back and sides over an Adirondack spruce top.

Adirondack is stiffer and more responsive at lower deflections than Sitka, which suits the reduced body volume of a parlour. The maple back and sides give the instrument a clear, present midrange without softening.

The client wanted something that could survive touring conditions and sound right in a hotel room at midnight. This is that guitar.
