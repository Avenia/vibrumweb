Cedar over walnut is an underused combination. The cedar top opens up quickly, responds to a light touch, and pairs with the walnut's midrange density to produce something warm but never woolly.

The OM body keeps the player close to the guitar. The modified V neck is a nod to pre-war ergonomics — comfortable for both fingerstyle and flatpicking. Ebony board, bone nut, French polish on the neck heel.

No electronics. This one is for rooms, not stages.
