The Fieldstone is the kind of guitar that rewards being played hard. A full-depth dreadnought with a Sitka top — stiff, clear, loud. Indian rosewood back and sides add warmth in the low mids without muddying the fundamental.

Waverly tuners, bone nut, French-polished mahogany neck. The D-shape profile feels natural after two hours of strumming.

Sold to a working folk musician in 2022. This one has miles on it by now.
